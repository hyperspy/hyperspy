:func:`~.api.plot.plot_spectra` (``'overlap'``, ``'cascade'`` and
``'heatmap'`` styles) and :func:`~.api.plot.plot_roi_map` with
``single_figure=True`` render through the active plotting backend, so on
``anyplotlib`` they produce live figures; the ``'mosaic'`` style and
``legend_picking`` remain matplotlib-only. Markers built from a raw
:class:`matplotlib.collections.Collection` subclass, and
:meth:`~.api.plot.markers.Markers.set_ScalarMappable_array`, raise
``BackendCapabilityError`` on a non-matplotlib backend instead of an opaque
``AttributeError``.
