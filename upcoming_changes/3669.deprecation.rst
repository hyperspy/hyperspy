Deprecate ``factors`` and ``loadings`` attributes of
:class:`~.learn.LearningResults` in favour of ``components`` and
``scores``. Similarly, ``bss_factors`` and ``bss_loadings`` are deprecated
in favour of ``bss_components`` and ``bss_scores``, and ``on_loadings``
in favour of ``on_scores``. The old attribute names still work but emit a
``VisibleDeprecationWarning`` and will be removed in 3.0.
Deprecate ``factors`` and ``loadings`` as accepted values for the
``target`` parameter of :meth:`~.api.signals.BaseSignal.normalize_decomposition_components`
and :meth:`~.api.signals.BaseSignal.normalize_bss_components` in favour of ``"components"``
and ``"scores"``. Similarly, deprecate ``"factors"`` and ``"loadings"`` as accepted
values for ``reverse_component_criterion`` in
:meth:`~.api.signals.BaseSignal.blind_source_separation` in favour of ``"components"`` and
``"scores"``. Deprecate the ``on_loadings`` keyword argument of
:meth:`~.api.signals.BaseSignal.blind_source_separation` in favour of ``on_scores``, and
the ``factor_prefix``/``loading_prefix`` keyword arguments of
:meth:`~.api.signals.BaseSignal.export_decomposition_results` and
:meth:`~.api.signals.BaseSignal.export_bss_results` in favour of ``component_prefix``/
``score_prefix``. The old values and keyword argument names still work but emit a
``VisibleDeprecationWarning`` and will be removed in 3.0.
