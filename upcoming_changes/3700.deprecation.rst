Deprecate ``factors`` and ``loadings`` attributes of
:class:`~.learn._mva.LearningResults` in favour of ``components`` and
``scores``. Similarly, ``bss_factors`` and ``bss_loadings`` are deprecated
in favour of ``bss_components`` and ``bss_scores``, and ``on_loadings``
in favour of ``on_scores``. The old attribute names still work but emit a
:class:`~.exceptions.VisibleDeprecationWarning` and will be removed in 3.0.
